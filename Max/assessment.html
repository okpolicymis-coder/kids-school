Part 1: Reading Fluency & Comprehension

What You're Learning: Reading is about two things: saying the words out loud (fluency) AND understanding what you read (comprehension). Today, you'll read a passage and then answer questions by finding where the answers are IN the text. This teaches you to be a "text detective" — looking for clues in the paragraph that prove your answer is correct.

Why it matters: Good readers don't just memorize facts. They can point to the exact sentence that proves something is true. This is a superpower in school!

Part 2: Spelling & Patterns

What You're Learning: Spelling isn't random memorization. Words follow patterns and rules. When you spell a word, you're using patterns you've learned (like knowing that "-tion" sounds like "shun", or that "running" doubles the N). Today, you'll hear words and spell them based on what you hear and what patterns you know.

Why it matters: Understanding patterns helps you spell NEW words you've never seen before, not just the ones you memorized!

Part 3: Writing & Sentence Building

What You're Learning: A complete sentence has three parts:
(1) a subject (WHO is doing something)
(2) an action word or verb (WHAT they're doing)
(3) a complete thought (it makes sense on its own)

Today, you'll build 5 sentences by answering specific questions about your favorite hobby.

Why it matters: Writing is how you share your ideas. If your sentences are complete, people understand you!

📚 Here's what makes a COMPLETE SENTENCE:

✅ Complete Sentences

"I play soccer."

Subject: "I"
Verb: "play"
Complete thought: Makes sense!
"Dogs are very friendly."

Subject: "Dogs"
Verb: "are"
Complete thought: Makes sense!
"She writes stories every day."

Subject: "She"
Verb: "writes"
Complete thought: Makes sense!

❌ NOT Complete Sentences

"Playing soccer."

Missing: WHO is playing? We don't know!
"Are very friendly."

Missing: Who or what is friendly? We don't know!
"Every day."

Missing: Who? What are they doing? Incomplete thought!
