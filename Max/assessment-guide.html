Part 1: Reading Fluency Check

⏱️ 8 minutes

Goal

See how Max decodes, handles multisyllabic words, and manages attention.

What to Do

  1. Give Max a grade-level paragraph (6th–7th grade nonfiction works best)
  2. Ask him to read aloud for one minute
  3. Let him finish the paragraph silently
  4. Ask him to tell you what it was about in 2–3 sentences

What to Observe

  • Does he guess at words?
  • Does he skip endings (-ed, -ing, -s)?
  • Does he lose his place?
  • Does he sound choppy or monotone?
  • Does he self-correct?

Data You'll Collect

Fluency + comprehension + working memory data in under 10 minutes

Part 2: Spelling Diagnostic (THE MOST IMPORTANT)

⏱️ 12 minutes

Goal

Map Max's spelling patterns to know exactly where to target instruction.

The Words Max Will Spell

decision

bicycle

discover

transport

Why These Words?

They target every major pattern 6th–7th graders struggle with:

  • -tion, -sion (action, decision)
  • -cial (special)
  • -ture (picture)
  • Soft c/g (bicycle, energy)
  • Doubling rules (running)
  • Silent-e (complete)
  • Schwa vowels (family)
  • Prefixes/suffixes & Latin roots

What to Observe

  • Does he spell phonetically? (e.g., "akshun" for "action")
  • Does he drop vowels?
  • Does he confuse soft c/g?
  • Does he over- or under-double consonants?
  • Does he show any awareness of meaning (roots/prefixes)?

Part 3: Writing Sample

⏱️ 10 minutes

Prompt for Max

"Write a short paragraph about your favorite game, show, or hobby. At least 5 sentences."

What to Observe

  • Sentence boundaries
  • Capitalization
  • Spelling in context
  • Organization
  • Attitude/avoidance behaviors
  • Can he sustain writing for 10 minutes?

☕ 15-Minute BREAK

This is important! Especially for kids with gaming-related dopamine patterns.

Break Rules

  • ✗ No screens
  • ✓ Snack + movement
  • ✓ Something sensory-resetting (walk, trampoline, pet the dog, stretch)
  • ✓ Keep it predictable: "We start again at [TIME]"

📊 What You've Learned

By the end of the first hour, you'll know whether Max struggles with:

1. Phonological decoding
(reading aloud is choppy)
2. Orthographic mapping
(spelling patterns don't stick)
3. Working memory
(he forgets instructions mid-task)
4. Executive function
(attitude, avoidance, shutdowns)
5. Motivation/dopamine imbalance
(common in gaming-heavy kids)
6. Writing stamina
(10 minutes feels impossible)

This Helps You Decide Whether You Need

  • Structured literacy
  • Executive function supports
  • Therapy for gaming addiction
  • Or a combination of all three
